About the Game

Start small. Collect rare cards. Build your business.

What is Card Rush?

Card Rush is a trading card collecting and business simulation game where players start as small collectors and work their way up to owning their own card store.

Players explore different locations, search for rare trading card products, compete with scalpers, and buy and sell cards to earn money.

As players progress, they can grow their collections, unlock new opportunities, and eventually open their own business.

Gameplay Features

Collect Trading Cards

Purchase card packs and products to collect cards of different rarities. Search for valuable cards and work toward completing your collection.

Compete with Scalpers

Visit stores to search for popular and limited products before they sell out. Compete against scalpers and other collectors to find valuable products.

Buy, Sell, and Trade

Earn money by buying, selling, and trading cards. Use your earnings to purchase more products and expand your inventory.

Open Your Own Store

Work your way up from a collector to a business owner. Open your own trading card store and manage your products, sales, and customers.

Game Progression

Players will progress through different stages as they grow their collection and build their business.

Progression Stage Player Activities Goals
Beginner Collector Purchase card packs and collect trading cards. Build a personal collection.
Product Hunter Visit stores and compete with scalpers. Find rare and limited products.
Card Reseller Buy, sell, and trade cards. Earn money and expand inventory.
Store Owner Open and manage a trading card store. Grow a successful business.

Game Development

Card Rush is currently a game concept focused on trading card collecting, exploration, and business management.

More information about the game world, artwork, and features will be added as development continues.

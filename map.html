The Card Rush World

Card Rush takes place in a city where players explore different locations to search for trading cards and collectible products.

Each location offers different opportunities. Some stores have limited products, while other areas allow players to buy, sell, and trade with other collectors.

Explore Different Locations

Retail Stores

Visit retail stores to search for trading card products. Popular items may sell out quickly, so players need to compete with other customers and scalpers.

Local Card Shops

Visit local card shops to discover rare cards, browse collections, and trade with other collectors.

Marketplace

Buy and sell cards in the marketplace. Compare prices and search for products that could increase in value.

Your Card Store

As players progress, they can unlock their own store. Manage products, attract customers, and expand the business.

Why Exploration Matters

Exploring the city is an important part of Card Rush. Players will need to visit different locations to find products and grow their collections.

Some products may be harder to find than others, which encourages players to explore more areas and look for new opportunities.
